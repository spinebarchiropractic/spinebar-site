# Spine Bar Chiropractic — Comprehensive SEO Audit

Audit date: September 22, 2026  
Production domain: https://www.spinebar.com/  
Scope: technical SEO, indexing, on-page content, structured data, internal linking, local entity consistency, reputation signals, performance, conversion measurement, and off-site authority.

## Executive finding

Spine Bar has a strong technical base for a young local practice: 21 canonical URLs, a valid sitemap and robots file, dedicated service and condition pages, crawlable internal links, one H1 per page, descriptive metadata, HTTPS, compression, structured data, and a verified business/practitioner identity.

The largest remaining growth constraints are not keyword placement. They are:

1. Google is still surfacing an older crawl of the conditions page.
2. The non-`www` domain currently redirects with a temporary 307 instead of a permanent 301/308.
3. Most inner pages are only about 180–205 words and need more original, clinically reviewed depth.
4. Search Console and conversion data are not available in the repository, so impressions, queries, indexing exclusions, calls, and bookings cannot yet be optimized from current evidence.
5. Local prominence is still limited by citation coverage, review velocity, local links, and stale practitioner records elsewhere on the web.

## Technical SEO

### Passing

- Preferred canonical host is `https://www.spinebar.com/`.
- HTTPS and HSTS are active.
- HTML is Brotli-compressed in production.
- All 21 sitemap URLs map to real local pages.
- `robots.txt` allows crawling and points to the canonical sitemap.
- All indexable pages have self-referencing canonical links.
- All audited pages have exactly one H1.
- All JSON-LD blocks parse successfully.
- The custom 404 returns HTTP 404 and includes `noindex,nofollow`.
- Retired `/new-patient`, `/blog`, and `/spine-bar` routes permanently redirect to relevant replacements.
- No broken internal links were found.

### Needs attention

- `https://spinebar.com/` currently returns a 307 redirect. Google treats 307 as temporary; a permanent 301 or 308 is preferred for canonical consolidation.
- `http://spinebar.com/` currently takes two hops to reach the preferred URL. It should go directly to `https://www.spinebar.com/` when domain routing permits.
- Public search results are still showing conditions-page copy crawled roughly three months ago. Submit the updated sitemap and request indexing for the homepage, services hub, conditions hub, about page, and highest-value detail pages.
- Current Core Web Vitals field data could not be retrieved from the public PageSpeed API because that endpoint returned a quota error. Use Vercel Speed Insights and Search Console's Core Web Vitals report as the authoritative field sources.

## Local SEO

Google states that local results are primarily based on relevance, distance, and prominence. Distance cannot be optimized. Relevance and prominence can.

### Verified public signals

- Google Business Profile: 5.0 rating from 26 reviews, primary public category shown as Chiropractor, address and booking action present.
- Zocdoc existing-patient profile: 4.78 from 106 reviews, 91% five-star feedback, and 95% of patients waiting under 30 minutes.
- NPPES organization and practitioner records match the current phone and North Hollywood postal address.

### Priority local actions

1. Keep the Google profile name, primary category, address, phone, website, appointment URL, hours, and services identical to the website.
2. Add fresh office and practitioner photos regularly, including exterior/entrance images that help visitors recognize the location.
3. Ask real patients for honest Google reviews using Google's direct review link. Do not incentivize or gate reviews.
4. Reply to every review without mentioning private health information.
5. Claim and maintain Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Healthgrades, WebMD/Vitals, and high-quality local directories.
6. Correct stale practitioner affiliations where directory owners allow updates. Public search still exposes historical Glendale, San Fernando, and Rowland Heights associations.
7. Earn genuine local links and mentions from neighborhood organizations, gyms, attorneys, prenatal professionals, sports groups, and community publications where a real relationship exists.
8. Track Maps performance from multiple nearby grid points; a search from the office is not representative of the whole service area.

## Measurement and conversion SEO

The site includes Vercel Speed Insights but no visible repository configuration for current Search Console reporting or booking/call/email/directions conversion events.

Required measurement stack:

- Google Search Console property for the canonical `www` site or domain property.
- Submitted sitemap and URL Inspection for priority pages.
- Monthly export of queries, pages, countries/devices, indexing status, and Core Web Vitals.
- Conversion tracking for booking-link clicks, phone clicks, email clicks, directions clicks, and completed scheduling if ChiroTouch supports a return or confirmation signal.
- Google Business Profile performance tracking for searches, views, calls, website clicks, direction requests, and bookings.
