Wellness Chiropractic Care

Some patients choose periodic care after an acute concern has improved. Wellness visits should still be purposeful: goals, frequency, and treatment are reviewed rather than assumed, and recommendations change as your needs change.

Care built around your assessment

No technique is appropriate for every person or every presentation. Dr. Chakrian begins with your history and relevant examination findings, explains the options, and adapts care to your comfort and goals.

What this service may include

  • Review of current function, activity, and any new concerns
  • Focused reassessment before hands-on care
  • Simple guidance for movement, recovery, and habits when relevant

When it may be a useful fit

  • You want periodic movement-focused check-ins
  • Your work, training, or daily routine creates recurring stiffness
  • You prefer an individualized schedule rather than an automatic long-term plan
Clinical note: Services are recommended only after an appropriate assessment. Results vary, and chiropractic care does not replace emergency or medically necessary evaluation.
