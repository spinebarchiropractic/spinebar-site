Soft Tissue Therapy

Muscles, tendons, fascia, and surrounding tissues can influence comfort and movement. Soft tissue therapy may be used alongside joint care when examination findings suggest that tension, sensitivity, or load tolerance is part of the pattern.

Care built around your assessment

No technique is appropriate for every person or every presentation. Dr. Chakrian begins with your history and relevant examination findings, explains the options, and adapts care to your comfort and goals.

What this service may include

  • Hands-on assessment of the involved area and related movement
  • Targeted manual techniques selected for comfort and clinical relevance
  • Practical movement guidance when it supports the goals of care

When it may be a useful fit

  • Muscular tightness accompanies a neck, back, shoulder, or hip concern
  • Training or repetitive activity has affected mobility or recovery
  • A combined joint and soft-tissue approach fits the examination findings
Clinical note: Services are recommended only after an appropriate assessment. Results vary, and chiropractic care does not replace emergency or medically necessary evaluation.
