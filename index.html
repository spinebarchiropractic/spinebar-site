07 Good to know

Questions,
answered clearly.

Understanding your care is part of receiving good care. Here are the questions we hear most.

What should I expect at my first visit?

A real conversation, a thorough physical assessment, a clear review of findings, and—in most cases—your first adjustment during the same 45–60 minute appointment.

Do you accept insurance?

We accept cash, personal injury liens, and Workers’ Compensation. We can also provide a detailed superbill for PPO reimbursement. Call us and we’ll help clarify your specific situation.

Will an adjustment hurt?

Care is adapted to your comfort, history, and presentation. Many patients experience immediate relief or easier motion. We explain what we’re doing and check in throughout your visit.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed to book a chiropractic visit. If we find that another type of care is more appropriate, we’ll tell you honestly and help point you in the right direction.

What should I wear?

Wear relaxed clothing you can move in—athletic wear, shorts, or anything loose-fitting works well. Bring prior imaging if you have anything relevant and recent.
