Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens when you use spinebar.com, contact Spine Bar by email, or follow a link to an outside service.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Information you choose to send

The question form on this website prepares an email in your device’s email application. The website does not directly submit or store the form entry. If you send the email, your message and contact details travel through your email provider and are received by Spine Bar’s email provider.

Please do not send medical records, insurance identifiers, detailed health histories, or emergency information through this form or ordinary email. For an emergency, call 911 or seek immediate medical care.

Appointments and outside services

Appointment links take you to an outside scheduling service. Information you provide there is handled under that service’s own privacy practices. The site also links to services such as Google Maps, Instagram, and review or scheduling platforms; those providers may collect information when you visit them.

Basic website data

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may process basic technical information—such as IP address, device or browser details, requested pages, and timing data—to deliver, secure, and measure the site. Spine Bar uses Vercel Speed Insights to understand general site performance.

How information is used

Information you send is used to respond to your question, help arrange care, maintain business records, and meet legal or safety obligations. Spine Bar does not intentionally sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your choices

You can browse the public pages without using the question form. You may contact Spine Bar to ask about personal information you previously sent or to request a correction or deletion, subject to any legal or record-retention requirements.

Contact

Questions about this notice may be sent to info@spinebar.com or discussed by phone at 747-774-7144.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the website or its services change. The date above identifies the current version.
