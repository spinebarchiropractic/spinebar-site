Hip Pain

Hip discomfort can come from the joint, surrounding muscles, the pelvis, or the lower back. Looking at the full movement chain helps clarify what may be contributing and which conservative options make sense.

Understanding the pattern

Symptoms can have more than one possible source. An in-person assessment helps connect what you feel with your history, movement, and relevant examination findings.

Common reasons people seek an evaluation

  • Pain near the side, front, or back of the hip
  • Discomfort with walking, stairs, sitting, or training
  • Hip symptoms that occur alongside lower-back stiffness

What your first visit may include

  • Comparison of hip, pelvic, and lumbar movement
  • Relevant orthopedic testing and hands-on assessment
  • A plan based on the suspected source and your activity goals
Medical note: This page is educational and is not a diagnosis. Seek urgent medical care for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, new weakness, loss of bowel or bladder control, saddle numbness, major trauma, chest pain, or other emergency concerns.
