Headaches & Migraines

Headaches have many possible causes. Some patterns are influenced by the neck, upper back, jaw, stress, or sustained posture, while others require medical evaluation. A careful history helps determine the safest next step.

Understanding the pattern

Symptoms can have more than one possible source. An in-person assessment helps connect what you feel with your history, movement, and relevant examination findings.

Common reasons people seek an evaluation

  • Head pain that begins near the neck or base of the skull
  • Headaches associated with neck stiffness or sustained posture
  • Recurring tension patterns that affect concentration or activity

What your first visit may include

  • Screening for history or symptoms that require medical referral
  • Assessment of neck motion, upper-back mechanics, and muscle tension
  • Conservative care only when the presentation is appropriate
Medical note: This page is educational and is not a diagnosis. Seek urgent medical care for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, new weakness, loss of bowel or bladder control, saddle numbness, major trauma, chest pain, or other emergency concerns.
