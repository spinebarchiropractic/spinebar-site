Sciatica

Sciatica describes symptoms that travel along a nerve pathway, but it does not identify the source by itself. Irritation may involve the lower back, pelvis, or surrounding soft tissue, so locating the most likely driver matters.

Understanding the pattern

Symptoms can have more than one possible source. An in-person assessment helps connect what you feel with your history, movement, and relevant examination findings.

Common reasons people seek an evaluation

  • Pain that travels from the lower back or buttock into the leg
  • Tingling, numbness, or sensitivity along part of the leg
  • Symptoms that change with sitting, bending, walking, or position

What your first visit may include

  • Neurologic and orthopedic screening appropriate to your symptoms
  • Assessment of lumbar, pelvic, hip, and soft-tissue mechanics
  • Honest guidance about whether conservative care is appropriate
Medical note: This page is educational and is not a diagnosis. Seek urgent medical care for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, new weakness, loss of bowel or bladder control, saddle numbness, major trauma, chest pain, or other emergency concerns.
